ProofMeet Field Testing Guide
For Participants, Court Representatives, and Meeting Hosts

Last Updated: February 10, 2026
Version: 3.0.0

---

INTRODUCTION

Thank you for testing ProofMeet! This system helps track meeting attendance for court compliance. This guide will walk you through everything you need to test.

What you will test:
1. Attending court-created test meetings
2. Finding and joining real recovery meetings (AA, NA, SMART, etc.)
3. Viewing court cards
4. Downloading proof of attendance

---

GETTING STARTED

Before You Begin:

1. Create your account
   - Visit the ProofMeet website
   - Choose your role: Participant, Court Representative, or Host
   - Complete registration with your email and password
   - Verify your email if required

2. Login to your account
   - Use your email and password
   - You should see your dashboard

3. Check your dashboard
   - Participants: You should see "Find Meetings" and "My Attendance"
   - Court Representatives: You should see "Create Test Meeting" and "Participants"

---

TEST 1: COURT-CREATED TEST MEETINGS

This test uses meetings created by Court Representatives specifically for testing. This is the recommended way to start testing the system.

FOR COURT REPRESENTATIVES: Creating a Test Meeting

Step 1: Create the Meeting

1. Login to your Court Representative account
2. Go to your dashboard
3. Click "Create Test Meeting" or similar button
4. Fill in the meeting details:
   - Topic: Give it a clear name like "Test Meeting - [Your Name]"
   - Duration: Set to 10-15 minutes for quick testing
   - Start time: Set to start in 2-5 minutes (or choose a specific date/time)
   - Timezone: Confirm your timezone is correct

5. Click "Create Meeting"

What you should see:
- Success message confirming the meeting was created
- Meeting details displayed including:
  - Meeting topic
  - Join URL
  - Meeting password (if applicable)
  - Start time
  - Duration

6. Copy the Join URL
7. Share the Join URL with your test participants

What to report:
- If meeting creation fails
- If Join URL is not displayed
- If meeting details are incorrect
- Any error messages

Step 2: Monitor the Meeting

8. After participants join, check your dashboard
9. Look for the meeting in your active meetings list
10. You should see participant attendance data after the meeting ends

What you should see:
- List of participants who joined
- Attendance percentages
- Duration for each participant
- Compliance status (Passed/Failed)

What to report:
- If participant data doesn't appear after the meeting
- If attendance percentages are incorrect
- If you can't view compliance details

FOR PARTICIPANTS: Joining a Test Meeting

Step 1: Get the Meeting Details

1. Ask your Court Representative for the Join URL
2. Make sure you know when the meeting starts

Step 2: Join via ProofMeet

3. Login to your ProofMeet participant account
4. Go to "Find Meetings" or "Available Meetings"
5. Look for the test meeting in the list OR
6. Use "Join Meeting by ID" if you have the Zoom Meeting ID
7. Click "Join" next to the meeting

What you should see:
- Confirmation that attendance tracking has started
- The Zoom meeting link
- Your dashboard updates to show you are in a meeting

What to report:
- If you cannot find the test meeting
- If the "Join" button doesn't work
- If you don't see confirmation that tracking started
- Any error messages

Step 3: Join the Zoom Meeting

8. Click the Zoom meeting link
9. Zoom should open (in browser or app)
10. Enter your name if prompted
11. Enter the meeting password if required
12. Join the meeting

What you should see:
- You successfully join the Zoom meeting
- Other participants may be present
- Your video and audio work

What to report:
- If the Zoom link doesn't work
- If you cannot join the Zoom meeting
- If there are connection issues

Step 4: Stay for the Duration

13. Keep your camera on during the meeting
14. Stay active (don't minimize or leave the meeting window for long periods)
15. Attend for the full duration if possible (at least 80% of meeting time for passing attendance)
16. Leave when the meeting ends or when you need to

What to report:
- Nothing needed here unless you have technical issues

Step 5: Check Your Attendance

17. After leaving the Zoom meeting, return to ProofMeet
18. Wait 1-2 minutes for the system to process your attendance
19. Refresh your dashboard or click "Sync Latest Data" if available
20. Go to "My Attendance" or "Meeting History"

What you should see:
- Your test meeting listed
- Status showing "COMPLETED"
- Total duration (how long you attended)
- Attendance percentage (should be 80%+ if you stayed for the full meeting)
- A court card should be generated

What to report:
- If the meeting doesn't appear in your history
- If attendance percentage is incorrect
- If duration is way off
- If status shows something other than "COMPLETED" after the meeting ends
- If no court card is generated

Step 6: View and Download Your Court Card

21. Find your completed meeting in "My Attendance"
22. Look for a "View" button or eye icon next to the meeting
23. Click to view the court card

What you should see:
- A popup or page showing your court card details:
  - Meeting name
  - Program type
  - Date attended
  - Join time and leave time
  - Total duration
  - Attendance percentage
  - Compliance status (Passed/Failed)
  - Any violations or notes

24. Look for a "Download PDF" button
25. Click to download the PDF

What you should see:
- PDF file downloads to your computer
- PDF contains all the court card information
- PDF is properly formatted

What to report:
- If you cannot view the court card
- If "Download PDF" button is missing or doesn't work
- If PDF is blank or improperly formatted
- If information in the court card is incorrect

TEST 2: REAL RECOVERY MEETINGS

This test uses actual AA, NA, SMART Recovery, and other recovery meetings happening online. Only test this when you want to attend a real meeting.

FOR PARTICIPANTS: Finding and Joining Real Meetings

Step 1: Search for Meetings

1. Login to ProofMeet with your participant account
2. Click "Find Meetings" or "Recovery Meeting Directory"
3. Look at the list of available meetings

What you should see:
- A list of recovery meetings
- By default, 9 meetings are shown
- Each meeting displays:
  - Meeting name
  - Program type (AA, NA, SMART, etc.)
  - Day and time
  - Zoom meeting ID
  - "Join" button

4. Try using the search filters:
   - Category filter: Select a specific program (AA, NA, etc.)
   - Date filter: Select a day of the week
   - Time filter: Adjust the time range slider
   - Timezone: Select your timezone to see meetings in your local time
   - Zoom Meeting ID: Search for a specific meeting if you know the ID

5. Click "Clear Filters" to reset
6. If more than 9 meetings are available, look for a "Show All Meetings" button

What you should see:
- Filters update the meeting list
- Meeting times adjust when you change timezone
- Zoom ID search finds the specific meeting
- "Show All Meetings" displays more meetings

What to report:
- If no meetings are displayed
- If filters don't work correctly
- If timezone changes don't update meeting times
- If search by Zoom ID doesn't work
- If page doesn't load or shows errors

Step 2: Join a Real Meeting

Important: These are REAL recovery meetings with real people. Be respectful of the meeting format and participants.

7. Choose a meeting that is happening soon or at a time you want to attend
8. Click the "Join" button next to the meeting
9. Confirm that you want to join

What you should see:
- Confirmation that attendance tracking has started
- Zoom meeting link
- Meeting details on your dashboard

10. Click the Zoom meeting link
11. Join the Zoom meeting as described in Test 1
12. Attend the meeting respectfully
13. Keep your camera on
14. Stay for at least 80% of the meeting duration to pass compliance

What to report:
- If you cannot join the meeting
- If tracking doesn't start
- If Zoom link is incorrect or broken

Step 3: Complete and Check Attendance

15. After the meeting ends, leave the Zoom meeting
16. Return to ProofMeet
17. Wait 1-2 minutes for processing
18. Check your "My Attendance" section

What you should see:
- Meeting listed in your history
- Status: COMPLETED
- Correct duration and attendance percentage
- Court card generated

19. View and download the court card as described in Test 1

What to report:
- Same reporting criteria as Test 1
- Any discrepancies in meeting information
- If the meeting was not a valid recovery meeting

COMPLETE TESTING SCENARIOS

To fully test the system, try these scenarios:

Scenario 1: Quick Test Meeting (Court Rep + Participant)
- Court Rep creates a 10-minute test meeting starting in 2 minutes
- Participant joins immediately
- Participant stays for full 10 minutes
- Both check that data is recorded correctly

Scenario 2: Partial Attendance Test (Participant)
- Join a test meeting
- Leave after only 30% of the duration
- Check that attendance shows as failed (below 80%)

Scenario 3: Real Meeting Attendance (Participant)
- Search and join a real AA or NA meeting
- Attend the full meeting
- Verify court card is generated with correct details

Scenario 4: Multiple Meetings (Participant)
- Attend 2-3 different meetings (test or real)
- Verify all appear in your attendance history
- Check that each has its own court card

Scenario 5: Filter Testing (Participant)
- Test each search filter option
- Verify results update correctly
- Check that combining filters works

PROBLEMS TO REPORT

Report any of these issues:

Login and Account Issues:
- Cannot create account
- Cannot login
- Password reset doesn't work
- Email verification fails

Meeting Discovery Issues:
- No meetings are displayed
- Search filters don't work
- Wrong meetings show up when filtering
- Timezone changes don't update meeting times
- "Show All Meetings" doesn't work

Joining Meeting Issues:
- "Join" button doesn't work
- Tracking doesn't start
- Zoom link is broken or incorrect
- Cannot join the Zoom meeting
- System doesn't recognize you joined

Attendance Tracking Issues:
- Attendance data doesn't appear after meeting
- Duration is wildly incorrect
- Attendance percentage doesn't match reality
- Meeting status stuck on "IN PROGRESS"
- Meeting never shows as "COMPLETED"

Court Card Issues:
- Court card not generated
- Cannot view court card
- Court card has wrong information
- Meeting name or program is incorrect
- Times or duration are wrong
- "Download PDF" button doesn't work
- PDF is blank or improperly formatted

Performance Issues:
- Pages load very slowly
- System freezes or crashes
- Data takes too long to update
- Buttons don't respond

HOW TO REPORT ISSUES

When you find a problem, please provide:

1. Your Role
   - Participant, Court Rep, or Host

2. What You Were Doing
   - Example: "I was trying to join a test meeting"

3. What Happened
   - Example: "When I clicked Join, I got an error message"

4. What You Expected
   - Example: "I expected tracking to start and see a Zoom link"

5. Can You Reproduce It?
   - Try the same action again
   - Does the problem happen every time?

6. Screenshots (if possible)
   - Take a screenshot of any error messages
   - Take a screenshot of incorrect data

7. Details
   - Date and time the problem occurred
   - Your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc.)
   - Device (computer, tablet, phone)
   - Meeting ID if relevant
   - Any error messages (copy the exact text)

Where to Send Reports:
- Contact your system administrator
- Email the development team
- Use the feedback form if available
- Document in your testing notes

TIPS FOR SUCCESSFUL TESTING

Before Testing:
- Make sure you have a stable internet connection
- Use a computer (not a phone) for easier testing
- Have your Zoom app installed or use Zoom in browser
- Clear your browser cache if you have login issues
- Use Chrome or Firefox for best compatibility

During Testing:
- Test one scenario at a time
- Take notes as you go
- Take screenshots of anything unusual
- Wait a few minutes after meetings end for data to process
- Don't rush - let the system complete each step
- Try both successful scenarios and intentional failures

After Testing:
- Review all your attendance records
- Check that all court cards are accessible
- Download a few PDFs to verify they work
- Compile your feedback
- Note anything confusing or unclear

COMMON QUESTIONS

Q: How long does it take for attendance data to appear?
A: Usually 1-2 minutes after the meeting ends. Refresh your page if needed.

Q: What attendance percentage do I need to pass?
A: Typically 80% or higher. This means if a meeting is 60 minutes, you need to attend at least 48 minutes.

Q: Can I test without attending a full meeting?
A: Yes, you can test with short court-created meetings (10-15 minutes) made specifically for testing.

Q: Do I have to attend real AA/NA meetings to test that feature?
A: No, it's optional. Only test real meetings if you want to attend them anyway. Test meetings are sufficient for most testing.

Q: What if I don't have access to Zoom?
A: You need Zoom to test the full system. You can use the free Zoom app or Zoom in your browser.

Q: Can I delete test attendance records?
A: Check with your administrator. Test data may need to be kept or may be removable.

Q: What browsers work best?
A: Chrome and Firefox are recommended. Safari should work but may have occasional issues.

Q: Can I test on my phone?
A: The system should work on mobile, but testing on a computer is easier and recommended.

Q: What if my camera doesn't work?
A: You need a working camera for attendance tracking. Check your Zoom camera settings and browser permissions.

Q: How do I know if my court card is valid?
A: After download, open the PDF. It should contain all meeting details, your attendance metrics, and a card number.

Q: Can I attend the same meeting multiple times?
A: Yes, each attendance will be tracked separately and generate its own court card.

Q: What happens if I have technical issues during a real meeting?
A: The system tracks what it can. If you disconnected, it will show partial attendance. Your attendance percentage will reflect the actual time connected.
